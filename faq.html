Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions we get from prospective tenants.

What's included in rent?

All utilities — electricity, gas, water, sewage, trash, and internet — plus furniture already in your room (bed, desk, and a Smart TV in most listings). Nothing extra to set up.

How long is the lease?

Month-to-month. Either side can end it with 30 days' written notice — no long-term lock-in.

What's the application process?

Start with the Tenant Pre-Screening Questionnaire, then schedule a tour. If it's a fit, you'll complete a formal application with a background, credit, and eviction check — the same screening every housemate has already passed.

What's the security deposit?

Equal to one month's rent, due before move-in along with your first month's rent (or a prorated amount, if applicable).

How do I pay rent?

Zelle, RentRedi, or cash. Paper checks aren't accepted.

Are pets allowed?

No pets at any of our properties.

Can I have guests?

Overnight guests require landlord permission in advance. Full house rules are shared after your tour.

Where do you operate?

Washington, DC and Maryland.
