About MIT Secure Stay

Furnished, all-inclusive shared housing in DC and Maryland — built for people who need a real home fast.

What We Do

MIT Secure Stay operates furnished, all-inclusive shared housing across the DC/Maryland area. Every room comes with utilities, Wi-Fi, and furniture already in place — no setup, no separate utility accounts, no furniture shopping. You bring your bags and move in.

Who We're Built For

Our tenants are usually on a timeline: traveling nurses and medical staff between contract assignments, relocating professionals and J-1 visa holders without U.S. credit history, and contractors or corporate relocators in town for a defined stretch of work. If you need a private, furnished room without a year-long commitment or a from-scratch apartment setup, this is built for you.

How We Operate

  • Every applicant — including current housemates — goes through the same background, credit, and rental history screening.
  • Leases are month-to-month, with 30 days' written notice to end from either side.
  • Rent is collected through Zelle, RentRedi, or cash — no paper checks.
  • Properties are professionally managed, with responsive support after you move in.
