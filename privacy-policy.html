Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This document is periodically reviewed and may be updated to reflect changes in applicable law and business practices. Last updated: July 2026.

What We Collect

When you use our Tenant Pre-Screening Questionnaire, schedule a tour, or contact us by email, we collect the information you provide directly — such as your name, contact details, move-in timeline, and information relevant to rental screening (subject to your consent as part of the formal application process).

How We Use It

  • To respond to inquiries and schedule tours
  • To evaluate rental applications, including background, credit, and rental history checks with your authorization
  • To communicate about your tenancy if you move in

Third-Party Tools

We use third-party services — including Calendly for scheduling and Google Forms for pre-screening — which have their own privacy policies governing the data submitted through them.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to mitsecurestay@gmail.com.
